# The Story Behind Our Demo Records

## What 576 Client Demos Reveal About Demand, Capability Gaps, and Growth Opportunities for Manila CS

**Period Covered:** FY2024 – FH1 2026  
**Prepared for:** Manila CIQ Client Support Team  

**Source:** FY2024, FY2025, and FH12026 Manila CIQ CS Visit Reports

---

## Purpose

This analysis reviews 576 logged client demos to understand where client demand is concentrated, which product areas generate the most interest, and what opportunities exist to expand Manila's demo capability and ownership. The objective is not only to understand historical trends, but also to identify practical actions that can help Manila CIQ CS Team increase demo participation, strengthen product expertise, and improve client engagement.

| Year | Coverage | Logged demos |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 2024 | January–December | 289 |
| 2025 | January–December | 220 |
| 2026 | January–June (FH1) | 67 |
| **Total** | FY2024 – FH1 2026 | **576** |

---

## Executive Summary

### Why We Conducted This Study

Client demos are one of the strongest indicators of client engagement and product demand. By analyzing 576 demo records, we sought to understand who our clients are, what they want to learn, and how Manila CIQ CS Team can better position itself to support future opportunities.

### Key Findings

- **Client Training** accounted for 374 of 576 demos (65%).
- **Investment & Finance** professionals were the most common audience every year.
- **Excel Plug-in, Screening, and Company Data** consistently ranked among the most requested solution areas.
- **AI-related discussions** increased significantly from 45 mentions in 2024 to 124 in 2025.

### Why It Matters

The findings suggest that Manila CIQ CS Team has an opportunity to expand and strengthen the number of associates capable of independently running demos, particularly in high-demand workflows and products. Doing so can improve client coverage, reduce dependence on a small pool of specialists, and create more opportunities to support revenue-generating engagements.

## Action Items and Recommendations

### 1. Develop a Demo Playbook Through the Demo In Action Working Group

Establish a standardized Demo Playbook that equips associates with the resources needed to confidently conduct demos across different client scenarios. The playbook should include demo templates, recommended talking points, discovery questions, common client use cases, and workflow-specific guidance focused on the most frequently requested solutions and demo types. This will help ensure consistency in demo delivery while reducing preparation time for associates.

**Action Items:**

- Create standardized templates for the top demo types and key workflows.
- Develop solution-specific talking points for Excel, Screening, Company Data, Valuation, AI Tools, and other high-demand topics.
- Maintain a repository of sample demos and best practices accessible to all associates.
- Review and update the playbook quarterly based on client demand trends.

### 2. Strengthen Demo Readiness and Product Expertise

Increase the confidence and effectiveness of demo-certified associates by strengthening their understanding of the products, key workflows, and the business objectives of their audiences. Beyond product functionality, associates should be equipped to tailor demonstrations based on the client's role and use case.

**Action Items:**

- Conduct targeted training sessions on the most requested workflows and solutions.
- Introduce refresher sessions for demo-certified associates on a quarterly basis.
- Develop learning paths centered on the most common client personas, particularly Investment & Finance professionals.
- Expand shadowing and mentorship opportunities with experienced demo presenters.
- Incorporate mock demos and peer feedback sessions into the certification program.

### 3. Expand Demo Opportunities from Existing Accounts

Leverage repeat-engagement clients as a source of additional demo opportunities. Clients who have previously requested demos are often well-positioned for follow-up sessions that introduce complementary workflows, newly released capabilities, or deeper use cases.

**Action Items:**

- Establish a watchlist of accounts with recurring demo engagements.
- Review previous demo topics prior to client meetings to identify follow-up opportunities.
- Coordinate with Sales and Relationship Managers to proactively recommend relevant solutions.
- Track repeat-demo metrics and identify accounts with the highest growth potential.
- Develop account-specific suggestions for adjacent products and workflows.

### 4. Improve Demo Documentation Quality and Insights

Accurate documentation enables the team to better understand client demand patterns, identify future opportunities, and prepare associates for follow-up engagements. Improving the completeness and consistency of demo records will strengthen future analysis and decision-making.

**Action Items:**

- Require completion of Key Workflow, Solution Discussed, and Job Role fields before case closure.
- Standardize documentation guidelines across all teams.
- Conduct monthly quality reviews of demo records and provide feedback where needed.
- Publish dashboard metrics on documentation completeness and data quality.
- Use documented insights to guide future training priorities and Demo In Action Working Group initiatives.
